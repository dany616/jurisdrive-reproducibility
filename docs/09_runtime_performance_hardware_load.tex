\subsection{Runtime Performance and Hardware Load}

All N5--N6 runtime measurements were obtained on a workstation containing two
Intel Xeon Gold 6530 processors (64 physical cores and 128 logical processors
in total), 512~GB of installed host memory (251.379~GiB visible to the
benchmark under WSL), and two NVIDIA RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell GPUs with
32,623~MiB per device. The software environment comprised
Ubuntu 24.04.4 LTS under WSL2, Python 3.12.3, CARLA 0.9.13, CUDA 13.0.2, and
vLLM 0.23.0. Qwen3.5-35B-A3B-FP8 was served with tensor parallelism two, a
32,768-token context window, and a maximum of eight active sequences.

We balanced six accepted executable contracts across 200 measured CARLA runs
and evaluated the same 200 artifacts with N6. CARLA used 12 scenario-isolated
server processes, synchronous 0.05-s stepping, low-quality off-screen
rendering, 800$\times$450 RGB capture, and 80 or 120 frames depending on the
contract. All 200 runs completed and satisfied the hard collision constraints.
The measured phase required 91.78~min, corresponding to 2.179 runs/min; mean
and P95 per-run latency were 224.74 and 343.27~s.

For N6, 30 sequential requests yielded mean/P95 latency of 1.93/2.36~s. The
remaining 170 requests were issued asynchronously at concurrency eight and
achieved 1.320 requests/s and 5,402.6 tokens/s, with mean/P95 latency of
6.01/7.42~s. All 200 evaluations passed, with no manual-review flag.

Across 1,166 resource samples collected during CARLA execution, mean/P95/peak
host CPU utilization was 19.45/21.15/28.19\%. Aggregate CARLA process load
averaged 2,303.84\% of one logical processor, or 23.04 logical-core equivalents.
CARLA resident-set memory averaged 37.78~GiB and peaked at 45.70~GiB; total
host-memory use averaged 54.50~GiB (21.68\% of the WSL-visible allocation) and
peaked at 57.34~GiB (22.81\%). Mean GPU utilization remained below 0.03\%
during low-quality off-screen simulation.

The VLM phase showed the opposite profile. Across 36 samples, mean GPU
utilization was 66.19\% and 68.75\%, P95 utilization was 96\% on both devices,
and peak utilization was 99\% and 97\%. Mean memory allocation was 28.67~GiB
(89.99\%) and 28.26~GiB (88.71\%), while mean device power was 113.25 and
107.94~W. Host CPU utilization averaged only 2.95\%, and host-memory use
averaged 18.87~GiB (7.51\%). Thus, the 12-worker simulator is primarily
CPU- and host-memory-oriented, whereas N6 throughput is constrained by GPU
compute and memory occupancy.

CARLA server startup, map warm-up, and six VLM warm-ups were excluded from
measured latency. The cached VLM service restart required 293.43~s and was also
excluded. These values therefore characterize steady-state N5--N6 throughput,
not cold-start latency or an end-to-end N0--N6 rerun.
